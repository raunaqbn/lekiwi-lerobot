## Intent

A technical field explainer for the open-source repository and documentation site. Teach the architecture through one concrete tension: the operator wants rich live diagnostics and remote control, but the serial bus must remain deterministic and physically local. Calm, exact, visually confident, and honest about safety limits.

## Customizations

- Cover both leader and follower readings, state vocabulary, battery uncertainty, browser arm takeover, and the cloud/local deployment boundary.
- End with a concrete open-source quick start and physical safety reminder.

## Notes

- No stock footage or generic robot imagery; use invented wiring diagrams, register readouts, and a schematic LeKiwi silhouette.
- Do not claim that software controls replace a physical power cutoff.
- Do not imply that Vercel or Cloudflare serverless owns USB motors.
- English narration and captions, landscape website delivery.
