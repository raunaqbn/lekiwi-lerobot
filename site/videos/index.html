Chapter guide
00:00—00:12

Observed, not assumed

A socket can be connected while command bytes are idle. The app keeps online, powered, flowing, and safe as different statements.

00:12—00:28

One bus owner

The 30 Hz owner loop samples motors and publishes immutable snapshots. HTTP, SSE, cameras, and iOS never open the serial bus from request handlers.

00:28—00:46

Source-labeled truth

A roughly 5 V leader and a roughly 10–12.6 V follower can both be healthy. The display preserves source, profile, freshness, and expected topology.

00:46—01:03

Battery as a band

The estimate uses follower voltage samples and reports uncertainty. Encoder calibration cannot magically provide battery capacity or runtime.

01:03—01:24

Twin and takeover

The 3D twin maps current joint state to selectable robot parts. Dashboard arm takeover intentionally replaces manual leader goals until Stop releases it.

01:24—02:03

Cloud boundary and release

Cloudflare, Vercel, and Pages can host documentation or protect a tunnel. Serial ownership, watchdogs, and the control loop remain beside the robot.

Safety note: software controls are not an emergency stop. Begin with all wheels lifted, the arm envelope clear, and a physical power cutoff in reach.
