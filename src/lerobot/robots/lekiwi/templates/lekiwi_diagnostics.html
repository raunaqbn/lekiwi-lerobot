LeKiwi diagnostics

Launch the local service first.

This page talks to your robot through a small Python service. Opening the HTML file directly can’t access the motor bus.

python -m lerobot.robots.lekiwi.diagnostics

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8090. The app will look for the bus automatically.

How accurate is this?

Low confidence. The pack is 11.1 V nominal, 12.6 V full, and 57.72 Wh. Load, charging, temperature, and cell age all move the voltage. The motor calibration files contain joint ranges, not a battery discharge curve, so runtime is unavailable until current draw or a logged discharge session exists.
